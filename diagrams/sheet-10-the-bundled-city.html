ALTITUDE 2⅞ — inside the wire: what the bundler kept · sample-app-lit-vanilla, one bundle · REV B: recounted after the single-lit merge · 2026-08-17

GENERAL NOTES

Method: a generateBundle census on the real production vite build of sample-app-lit-vanilla — output chunk hashes identical to the deployed dist, so this is the deploy itself, not a reconstruction. Every rendered module is attributed to its package; footprint is post-tree-shake source kept, height is each module scaled by its own chunk's minify and gzip ratios — the honest wire share. Beside it, the repo's own codecov bundle-analysis probe (tools/bundle-probe, the <pkg>-<label>-esm series CI uploads) is recomputed locally to price each exported entry. This closes the survey as a quartet: what we wrote (7), what npm delivered (8), what the wire carries (9) — and now who actually occupies those bytes. Sheet 11 is this sheet's split view: the same probe cut by published package, every entry priced alone. REV B re-runs the identical census after the single-lit + lazy api-viewer merge (PR #618): 630,184 kept → 370,696 emitted → 119,906 gz, against rev A's 654,726 → 385,793 → 124,449.

The library is a skin over the machine. The biggest building in the bundle is not the router package — it is @uirouter/core at 27.1 KB gz, 23% of the wire. lit-ui-router itself is the small accent building: 4.8 KB, 4.0% — both figures a notch up from rev A (4.6 KB) because #590's assignHref landed between prints, not because of the dedupe. The two measurements still agree to within 10%: the codecov door prices the bare . entry at 5,308 gz (rev A 4,953 — same #590 growth), and the app's census pulls 4,772 of it.

The déjà vu is gone. Rev A's one redundancy tree-shaking could not reach — a second, complete lit 2.8.0 riding in with the docs-viewer stack, 5.2 KB of wire déjà vu — was a version split, so it took a dependency edit, not a bundler: PR #618 scopes a pnpm override (^3.3.3, a floor, not a pin) to @api-viewer/* and lit-dialog, and the census now counts one lit: 8.6 KB gz where two majors cost 12.5. The intentional lit-2 compat alias in packages/* is untouched — it is a test lane, and it never shipped.

Occupancy barely moved; arrival did. The same PR defers the api-viewer panel behind its feature flag, so marked, dompurify and @api-viewer — 27 KB gz of demo chrome — now ride a lazy api-docs chunk and the eager main chunk drops 34 → 7 KB gz. This sheet's census is deliberately blind to that: it counts who occupies the bundle, and the occupants are the same tenants in new rooms. The −4.5 KB on this sheet is the dedupe; the lazy split's win is drawn on sheet 9, where the critical path lives.

The lodash-es aftermath did not move. 7.5 KB gz, byte-for-byte the rev A figure (7,508 vs 7,505) — isEqual's _base* family still its own 4.2 KB chunk. Tree-shaking is working; this is what four imports truly cost.

The runtime tax is near-zero. All bundler machinery — oxc decorator helpers, vite's preload helper and polyfill, a stray tslib — totals 4.6 KB kept, 1.3 KB gz, 0.7% of the kept source. No duplicated-helper problem: exactly one oxc helper module ships.
