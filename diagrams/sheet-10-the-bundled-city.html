GENERAL NOTES

Method: a generateBundle census on the real production vite build of sample-app-lit-vanilla — output chunk hashes identical to the deployed dist, so this is the deploy itself, not a reconstruction. Every rendered module is attributed to its package; footprint is post-tree-shake source kept, height is each module scaled by its own chunk's minify and gzip ratios — the honest wire share. Beside it, the repo's own codecov bundle-analysis probe (tools/bundle-probe, the <pkg>-<label>-esm series CI uploads) is recomputed locally to price each exported entry. This closes the survey as a quartet: what we wrote (7), what npm delivered (8), what the wire carries (9) — and now who actually occupies those bytes. Sheet 11 is this sheet's split view: the same probe cut by published package, every entry priced alone.

The library is a skin over the machine. The biggest building in the bundle is not the router package — it is @uirouter/core at 28.8 KB gz, 26% of the wire. lit-ui-router itself is the small accent building: 4.6 KB, 3.7%. The two measurements agree to within 9%: the codecov door prices the bare . entry at 4,953 gz, and the app's census pulls 4,552 of it.

The demo chrome outweighs the thing it demos. The visualizer ships as a 24.7 KB prebundled file — opaque to tree-shaking, take-it-or-leave-it — and marked + dompurify add 22.9 KB so @api-viewer can render component API markdown: five times the router they document. The docs-viewer stack also drags in a second, complete lit — sheet 8's twin pairs survive bundling as 5.2 KB of wire déjà vu, the one redundancy tree-shaking cannot reach because it is a version split, not dead code.

The lodash-es aftermath, measured. 7.5 KB gz remains from four import sites — isEqual's 89-module _base* family is its own 4.2 KB chunk, with cloneDeep/get/set adding 3.3 KB more. Tree-shaking is working; this is what four imports truly cost, and the swap's −84% (sheet 9) came from shedding everything else.

The runtime tax is near-zero. All bundler machinery — oxc decorator helpers, vite's preload helper and polyfill, a stray tslib — totals 1.9 KB, 0.4% of the kept source. No duplicated-helper problem: exactly one oxc helper module ships.
