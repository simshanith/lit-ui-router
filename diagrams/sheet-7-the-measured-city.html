GENERAL NOTES

Method — one basis, two series. Counted from this repo's authored source at HEAD on 2026-08-16, the same basis as sheets 3 and 4: .ts/.tsx/.js/.jsx/.mjs under each member's source directory, excluding *.d.ts, *.test-d.ts, fixtures, dist/ and node_modules/; sloc is lines that are neither blank nor comment-only. Sheet 3 throws test code away; this sheet does not — anything matching *.{spec,test,cy}.* or living under specs/ test/ tests/ __tests__/ cypress/ is counted into a second series and drawn as that member's annex. 27 members: 182 authored files and 12,098 sloc of source, plus 76 files and 9,850 sloc of spec. The packages district reconciles exactly with sheet 3's source slab — 25 files, 2,568 lines.

Every shipped building still has an annex bigger than itself. lit-ui-router: 1,189 authored lines against 2,879 of spec (2.4×). ui-router-server: 1,141 vs 2,174 (1.9×). lit-ui-router-mobx: 133 vs 380 (2.9×). navigation-location-plugin is one 105-line file with seven spec files over it (3.9×). The test budget lives exactly where the repo says it should — in packages/*, and nowhere else at that ratio.

The roads are the new content, and every one is citable. Solid roads are build edges: packages/* into the sample apps, the apps into docs, @tools/oxc-emit into all four packages — each a workspace: dependency, each backed by turbo's build/docs tasks depending on ^build. Dashed soft roads are the test lane: @tools/dts-backtest depends on all four published packages and backtests their emitted d.ts against the TS 5.0 floor; @tools/lit-test-env and @tools/happy-dom run up into lit-ui-router's annex because they are its devDependencies and the harness its suites load; sample-app-lit-e2e depends on docs, and turbo's e2e depends on ^docs, so Cypress drives the shopfront build rather than a dev server. The accent road is the one that leaves a member twice — once from the source block and once from the spec annex — because the package's typecheck reads both: tsconfig.json includes src/**, specs and all (only the narrower typecheck:src excludes them), while the ^build:types dependency is what carries the result into the next district. One faint road remains: five instruments import @tools/shared; only the road to the largest is drawn, because drawing all five would turn the yard into hatching.

Severity in colour, mass in geometry — and they disagree. The tiers are sheet 3's, applied to whole members: @tools/release is solid red because it hosts published-diff, the only structure that halts a publish; red hatch marks the five members that can stop a pull request — Cypress e2e, dts-backtest, compat-guards, lit-test-env, happy-dom; accent hatch marks @tools/workers-builds, which gates the docs deploy and nothing earlier. Here the two encodings happen to agree once, on release, and disagree everywhere else: the harness that can stop every pull request in the repo is 8 authored lines of @tools/happy-dom, sitting on the drawing's minimum footprint.

What the districts say. The product is four modest buildings — 2,568 lines — against 4,684 in the instrument yard and 3,052 in the proving ground. The broadest source block on the sheet belongs to sample-app-shared, which ships to nobody and gates nothing — and the only footprint that outbuilds it anywhere on the sheet is lit-ui-router's spec annex. @tools/wintercg-globals is drawn on the floor with no mass at all: it is ambient types, and a census that hides its empty members is not a census.
