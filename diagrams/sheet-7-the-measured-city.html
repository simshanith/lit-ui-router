GENERAL NOTES

Method — one basis, two series. Every count on this sheet is read at build time from the checked-in plate diagrams/data/census-city.json, counted at origin/main @ b2338d0 (2026-09-05): .ts/.tsx/.js/.jsx/.mjs under each member's source directory, excluding *.d.ts, *.test-d.ts, fixtures, dist/ and node_modules/; sloc is scc's Code count — string-aware, so the interior of a template literal counts as code rather than as one line. Sheet 3 throws test code away; this sheet does not — anything matching *.{spec,test,cy}.* or living under specs/ test/ tests/ __tests__/ cypress/ is counted into a second series and drawn as that member's annex. 32 members: 221 authored files and 16,520 sloc of source, plus 96 files and 14,105 sloc of spec. The packages district is five buildings now, and it still reconciles exactly with sheet 3's source slab — 36 files, 3,520 lines — because both are queries over the same census rather than two hand counts.

Every shipped building still has an annex bigger than itself. lit-ui-router: 1,383 authored lines against 4,068 of spec (2.9×). ui-router-server: 1,141 vs 2,174 (1.9×). lit-ui-router-mobx: 176 vs 453 (2.6×). eslint-plugin-lit-ui-router, the newcomer: 715 vs 1038 (1.5×). navigation-location-plugin is one 105-line file with 7 spec files over it (3.9×). The test budget lives exactly where the repo says it should — in packages/*, and nowhere else at that ratio.

The roads are the new content, and every one is citable. Solid roads are build edges: packages/* into the sample apps, the apps into docs, @tools/oxc-emit into all five packages — each a workspace: dependency, each backed by turbo's build/docs tasks depending on ^build. Dashed soft roads are the test lane: @tools/dts-backtest depends on the four runtime packages and backtests their emitted d.ts against the TS 5.0 floor; @tools/lit-test-env and @tools/happy-dom run up into lit-ui-router's annex because they are its devDependencies and the harness its suites load; sample-app-lit-e2e depends on docs, and turbo's e2e depends on ^build and runs with docs#wrangler:dev, so Cypress drives the built shopfront under its worker rather than a vite dev server. The accent road is the one that leaves a member twice — once from the source block and once from the spec annex — because the package's typecheck reads both: tsconfig.json includes src/**, specs and all (only the narrower typecheck:src excludes them), while the ^build:types dependency is what carries the result into the next district. One faint road remains: eight instruments now import @tools/shared — src/globs.ts (#655) added the custom-element glob three lanes share; only the road to the largest is drawn, because drawing all eight would turn the yard into hatching.

Severity in colour, mass in geometry — and they disagree. The tiers are sheet 3's, applied to whole members: @tools/release is solid red because it hosts published-diff, the only structure that halts a publish; red hatch marks the six members that can stop a pull request — Cypress e2e, dts-backtest, compat-guards, lit-test-env, happy-dom, and now lint-elements, whose ratchet exits non-zero on a new warning; accent hatch marks @tools/workers-builds, which gates the docs deploy and nothing earlier. Here the two encodings happen to agree once, on release, and disagree everywhere else: the harness that can stop every pull request in the repo is 8 authored lines of @tools/happy-dom, sitting on the drawing's minimum footprint.

What the districts say. The product is 5 modest buildings — 3,520 lines — against 6,727 in the instrument yard and 3,156 in the proving ground. The broadest source block on the sheet is examples — 2,404 lines of stackblitz copies — with sample-app-shared, which ships to nobody and gates nothing, just behind at 2,211; the only footprint that outbuilds both is lit-ui-router's spec annex. @tools/wintercg-globals is drawn on the floor with no mass at all: it is ambient types, and a census that hides its empty members is not a census.

REV D — two things moved at once, and the plate keeps them apart. First the ruler: sloc is now scc 4.0.0's Code count rather than the old "neither blank nor comment-only" filter. Measured both ways over one identical file set — sheet 3 rev B's twenty-five source directories at today's HEAD — the old counter reads 11,560 and scc reads 11,658, about +0.9%. So roughly a hundred lines of the growth below is the tape measure, not the building. Second the city: fourteen days, one release (lit-ui-router@1.10.0, tagged 2026-08-17) and three new instruments. №28 @tools/lint-elements and №29 @tools/warn-lanes were both born 2026-08-31 (#639); №30 @tools/eslint-ts-parser was born 2026-08-16 (#557) and is the "28th member, not yet on any map" that plate 7B recorded — it can be placed now, and at 1 authored line it lands on the drawing's minimum footprint, the smallest thing in the yard. The yard is where the growth is: 16 members and 4,684 sloc become 20 and 6,727, and it stays the city's largest district by a wide margin.

Numbers by import, not by paste. Every count above and on the drawing is read from diagrams/data/census-city.json, the snapshot census-city.mjs writes out of the master per-file census; this file now holds placement, tiers and prose only. The plate is measured at a named ref rather than at whatever a working tree happens to hold — counted at origin/main @ b2338d0 (2026-09-05) — so a number here is citable to a committed file, and a member the sheet draws but the plate does not carry is a build error rather than a stale constant. Two things followed from the swap: the fifth published package, eslint-plugin-lit-ui-router (#676), gets a building for the first time — 9 files, 715 lines, with a 9-file annex over it — and several members moved because the basis did, most visibly @tools/oxc-emit, hand-kept at 3 files and 100 lines and actually 5 files and 228.
