GENERAL NOTES

This plate is the legibility companion to sheet 2. The exploded assembly (sheet 2, THE BRICK ASSEMBLY) shows the whole stack and where every brick falls; this plate isolates the couplings and draws each one at reading size. It deliberately returns to rev A’s spatial arrangement — core central, companions at its right, the server in a request lane below the no-DOM line — but renders the packages as shallow solids and spends the recovered space entirely on the joints.

Every joint is drawn disengaged. A seated plug hides both mating faces, so nothing here is seated: each stud on the wall stops 18px short of its plug’s mouth, the dashed centreline is the engagement, and the call that makes the coupling — stateRegistry.decorator('views', litViewsBuilder), urlService.listen()/.sync(), router.plugin(servicesPlugin), transitionService.onSuccess({}, update), globals.current/.params — is lettered on the connection itself, not in a schedule at the edge of the sheet.

One connection point is a seat, not a stud, and it is drawn as a keyed recess. A router holds exactly one location plugin, so ui-router-navigation-location-plugin does not add to the wall — it swaps: its keyed red plug approaches the LOCATION SEAT while a dashed ghost plug, core’s own pushStateLocation, leaves it. The rail above behaves the opposite way: router.plugin() is one method with any number of seats, which is where visualizer, sticky-states, dsr and rx would queue (sheet 4).

The one brick-to-brick coupling gets its own axis. lit-ui-router-mobx is the only companion that touches another companion: its upward socket waits under a stud on lit-ui-router’s underside — seekRouter(host), the bubbling ui-router-context event — and everything else it does is read-only against the wall: one memoised onSuccess hook on stud C, observations of globals on stud E, never a write.

The missing coupling is drawn only to be crossed out. ui-router-server takes no stud: @uirouter/core is an optional peer (peerDependenciesMeta), its default 'matcher' tier is dependency-free, and its 'simulate' tier lazily imports a wall of its own. Blocks are sized roughly by mass — front-face area ≈ 35 px² per sloc, from census-bricks.json counted at origin/main @ b2338d0 (the 2026-08-17 recount moved lit-ui-router to 1,325 sloc; it stands at 1,383 now) — with the two smallest companions (1×1 and 1×2) clamped up to a legible minimum, because at true scale they would be postage stamps, and their smallness is already sheet 2’s finding.
