GENERAL NOTES

Method — one pass, renames followed. Every date on this sheet is read at build time from the checked-in plate diagrams/data/census-weather.json, counted at origin/main @ b2338d0. Each file in the shared city universe was dated from a single git log --name-status -M pass over the history behind that ref, walked newest to oldest with rename chains followed backwards — equivalent to per-file --follow (spot-verified against it, including tools/shared/workspace.ts, which the plain path log mis-dates by a week) but one process instead of 317. Per file: first-commit date, last-touch date, touch count. Merge commits list no files under the default log, so a touch is a non-merge commit — the standard convention. TODAY is not a wall-clock date: it is the measured ref's own commit date, 2026-09-04, so every age and idle figure below is reproducible from the sha rather than from the day the sheet was drawn. Footprints come from sheet 7's plate, diagrams/data/census-city.json, reused as pure geometry so the two sheets reconcile by eye — and by count: 317 dated files here against the same 317 there.

The bands are campaigns, not round numbers. The age histogram is not a slope, it is three spikes: files born around day 411 (July 2025), around day 237 (January 2026), and within the last 63 days — with nothing between them, because the repo has source births or touches in only 5 of its 15 calendar months. So the map bins by season, where the distribution actually cuts: SEASON I, the port — 26 files that arrived 2025-07-21 with the upstream sample-app lineage; SEASON II, the winter works — 18 files including the typedoc plugin and the examples; SEASON III, the summer — 273 files, 86% of the city, none older than 2026-07-04. Churn tiers come from the per-block distribution the same way: touches-per-file still has a clean gap between 4.65 and 6.0, so HOT is ≥6; the per-file median is still 2, so COLD is below it. SEALED is idle >180 days — the idle histogram is empty from 63 to 229, and exactly the same 6 files sit beyond the gap.

The verdict: age and churn run opposite ways. Mean touches per file falls monotonically with youth — ×10.0 for the port cohort, ×5.8 for winter, ×2.3 for summer — and 131 of 317 files (41%) have been touched exactly once, ever. The port's masonry is not museum stone: ui-sref.ts is at ×18 with the last chisel-mark dated 2026-09-02, and the two most-weathered walls in the city are the port-era docs/.vitepress pair — config.ts at ×27 and vite.config.ts at ×22. What survives from the first day is precisely what keeps being worked.

What each district's weather says. The instrument yard — 6,727 sloc, the city's largest district by mass — logged zero touches before 2026 and is almost entirely summer stone: infrastructure arrived late, fast, and mostly settled on the first cut. Its one winter building is also the map's sealed wing: the typedoc plugin's symbols/ trio, untouched 239 days. The shopfront splits in two: docs runs the hottest multi-file block-average in the city (×8.5/f src) while examples holds the oldest untouched stone — two vite configs idle 247 days. And the smallest building on the map has the sharpest weather: navigation-location-plugin's single January wall has been chiselled 10 times, the highest per-file churn anywhere — matching its history as the extracted plugin that every routing change touches.

One number in the prose did move with the clock and is now derived rather than typed — the empty stretch the seal sits in reads 63 to 229 at this ref, where rev C printed 61 to 227.

Approximations, so the numbers stay honest. Dates are author dates (%as); on this repo's squash-merge convention they equal the merge day of the PR that landed the change. Rename detection is git's -M heuristic: a file moved and rewritten in one commit can read as a fresh birth — the July 2026 scripts/ → tools/ graduations are dated to that graduation where git saw no rename, which the yard's notes above already state as its story. Generated and vendored trees were never in the universe (the shared city rules); nothing else was excluded.
