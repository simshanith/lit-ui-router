GENERAL NOTES

Method: the same probe as sheet 10's doors strip, run to completion: every exported entry of the four publishable packages bundled alone with rolldown — minified, declared dependencies and peers external — which is the price a consumer's bundler pays at that door, and byte-for-byte the <pkg>-<label>-esm series CI uploads to codecov. Footprint is minified bytes, height gzipped. Sheet 10 is the unified view — one real app's bundle with all of these mixed by the bundler; this sheet cuts the same machinery by published package and entry point. The quarters don't sum and shouldn't: doors overlap (./pure is . minus registration), which is exactly what the split view exists to show.

The twin towers are one building with two doors. . and ./pure differ by 89 gzipped bytes — the whole cost of custom-element registration. The register economics run the other way: the two one-element doors sum to 3,338 gz while the umbrella ./register is 2,288, because each element door carries the shared element core with it. Take the umbrella or one element door; taking two per-element doors ships the core twice.

The server is a storefront, not a tower. Eight doors: an index that prices within 12 bytes of lit-ui-router's flagship (4,965 vs 4,953 — coincidence, but a tidy one), redirect and matcher wings, and four framework adapters — hono, fetch, vite, connect — packed within 72 bytes of one another: thin skins over one core. ./simulate, the test double, is 283 bytes.

Plugins price like footnotes. The entire mobx reactivity layer enters at 650 gz and the navigation-location plugin at 670 — each cheaper than the gap between ./register and its two element doors. The expensive thing in this family is never the adapter; it is the state machine they all defer to, and that machine (sheet 10's 28.8 KB @uirouter/core) is external here by design.

Why both views exist. The split view is the seller's ledger — what each door costs at the threshold. The unified view is the buyer's receipt — what one app's bundler actually made of them (it paid 4,552 of the flagship's 4,953). Codecov tracks the ledger per commit; sheet 10 audits the receipt.
