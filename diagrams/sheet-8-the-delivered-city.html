GENERAL NOTES

Method: the production closure of apps/sample-app-lit-vanilla (dependencies plus installed peers — pnpm auto-installs peers, so they are genuinely on disk), resolved through the pnpm store and measured as delivered: lines of .js/.ts/.css/.html per package directory, source maps and prose excluded, .d.ts drawn separately as the hatched annex. Workspace-linked members are measured by their built dist/ — the shape a registry install would deliver. Plenty of prior art measures this differently — bundlephobia and packagephobia weigh tarballs and install bytes, npmgraph draws the dependency graph — but this sheet measures what actually lands on disk, in files and lines, and stages it as a city with the consumer’s own building standing in it for scale. The closure is resolved and measured inside an installed and built archive of a named ref, not in a working tree, and written to the plate diagrams/data/census-nm.json — measured at origin/main @ b2338d0 (2026-09-05), closure of apps/sample-app-lit-vanilla — which every number below is read from.

The consumer is a doormat in its own city. The app brings 592 lines of its own and receives 177,163 delivered lines of code plus 39,942 of type declarations — 299× its own mass. The router this repo exists to ship, lit-ui-router, delivers 1,525 lines: still one of the smaller buildings on the skyline it anchors, and 116× smaller than the city it stands in.

The drawing changed the city — twice. Rev A drew lodash 4.18.1 as the tallest building on the skyline — 45,205 lines, 1,048 files, delivered for four imports (isEqual, cloneDeep, get, set). That finding became PR #604: the swap to lodash-es halved the building to 22,193 lines and 644 files, dropping it to fourth place behind dompurify, hono, and @uirouter/core. The bundler always tree-shook the wire cost — sheet 9 charts that collapse, 25 KB → 4 KB — but the delivered city is what installs, audits, and updates, and it is 23,000 lines lighter.

Rev C: the lit twins are gone. Rev B found the demo chrome shipping a second, complete lit — lit-dialog and the api-viewer panels hard-depend on lit ^2, so lit, lit-html, lit-element, and @lit/reactive-element were each delivered twice, twin pairs on the skyline. That finding became PR #618: scoped pnpm overrides (@api-viewer/docs>lit, @api-viewer/common>lit, lit-dialog>lit → ^3.3.3) retire the 2.8.0-era tree. Four buildings vanished — lit 2.8.0, lit-html 2.8.0, lit-element 3.3.3, @lit/reactive-element 1.6.3: 287 files and 14,114 lines of code, plus 7,781 d.ts lines — and the city shrank from 36 delivered packages to 32, 190,122 lines to 176,022. The same PR made the api-docs panel lazy-load, which is why sample-app-shared's dist grew 14 lines (3,776 → 3,790); every other surviving building measures exactly what it did in rev B. And hono (30,489 lines) still stands here because ui-router-server names it a peer: a server framework delivered into a client demo by peer auto-install.

Note the vertical scale: 1 px ≈ 250 lines against sheet 7’s ≈ 34. Drawn at the workspace’s own scale, even the halved lodash-es would stand 653 px tall — the compression is the finding.

Numbers by import, not by paste. Every count on the drawing and in this note is now read from diagrams/data/census-nm.json, the snapshot census-nm.mjs writes after installing and building the ref itself; this file holds the drawing order, the hand-placed districts and the prose only, and a package it draws that the plate does not carry — or a package the plate carries that no district draws — is a build error rather than a stale constant. The same 32 buildings stand: the set has not changed since the hand count, only the counts. lit-ui-router's own delivered shape moved most — 1,525 lines over 26 files against the 798 over 12 pasted here in rev C, several releases of dist ago (the plate reads lit-ui-router 1.11.2) — and the rest is drift in the registry: hono hono 4.13.5 delivers 30,731 where 4.13.1 delivered 30,489, sample-app-shared's dist is 3,962 where it was 3,790, and @oxc-project/runtime advanced three minors to @oxc-project/runtime 0.147.0 without changing a single line it delivers. The city totals 177,163 lines against rev C's hand-counted 176,022.
