GENERAL NOTES

Method — one meter per member, nothing installed, nothing left behind. Footprints, annexes and districts are sheet 7 rev B's census, unchanged, so the two plates overlay. Shadows were metered on 2026-08-17 at worktree HEAD 3557c29: the four packages by the repo's own turbo run test:coverage tasks (vitest v8 for three, node --test --experimental-test-coverage + rebase-lcov for ui-router-server); the eight node:test members by the same node flags passed on the command line; @tools/happy-dom by vitest run --coverage.enabled --coverage.provider=v8. No repo file was edited. Each meter reports lines on its own basis (v8 remaps to executable lines; node counts raw lines), so darkness percentages are per-meter and never summed across meters in the drawing — only the schedule's grand total does, and says so. Extent uses the atlas sloc rule over the code the meter actually saw; two members' source moved between the census and the metering and are daggered in the schedule.

The product is dark wall to wall — the budget lands where the house says. All four published packages meter 98.1–100% line, 91.7–100% branch, over 94–100% of their source. What little stands in the sun is entry barrels and interface.ts — 69 of lit-ui-router's 80 unlit sloc are type declarations, which no runtime meter can light. This is the priority made visible: library coverage outranks docs coverage, and the annexes sheet 7 drew at 1.9–3.9× their buildings turn out to buy near-total shadow.

The yard's habit: cores in shadow, wrappers in the sun. Four instruments repeat one pattern — @tools/release (23 .core.ts files at 98.4%, 21 CLI wrappers unlit), build_and_test (3 wrappers unlit), workers-builds (the 156-sloc trigger wrapper), lcov-rebase (the 17-sloc bin). 1,375 sloc of process-edge code is never loaded by any suite, while the logic behind it meters 96.7%. compat-guards is the pattern at its extreme: only ranges.ts is unit-lit; its six guard lanes run for real in CI, where no meter follows. And @tools/happy-dom is the survey's one genuine surprise, re-verified before inking: it has a suite and casts no shadow at all, because its spec is a conformance canary pointed at happy-dom upstream — its own append.ts is lit only inside lit-ui-router's annex, as borrowed light.

What the meter cannot say, the plate refuses to fake. The two demo apps and the Cypress host are hatched in accent, not banded: e2e light is real — the rig drives the built docs site — but no lcov leaves it, so their shadows are drawn at nominal reach and labelled unmetered. sample-app-shared runs 43 unit tests green, yet browser-mode vitest cannot fetch a coverage provider the repo never installed (@vitest/coverage-v8 is no devDependency of it); its shadow is an outline. Nine members — examples, the typedoc plugin, and seven instruments — cast nothing because nothing tests them; two of those (dts-backtest, lit-test-env) spend their working lives inside other members' suites and are red-badged with that caveat in the schedule.
