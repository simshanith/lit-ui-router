GENERAL NOTES

REV D — the footprints moved, the light did not. Sheet 7's census was re-taken on 2026-08-31 on a new sloc ruler (scc 4.0.0's Code count) and grew from 27 members to 30, so this plate's footprints, annexes and districts were refreshed to match — the two plates still overlay building for building. The light was not re-metered. This plate's 13-member, 5,539-line universe comes from bespoke census-shadow.mjs runs (nine of the thirteen members are metered by nothing the repo itself schedules), and re-running turbo run test:coverage reproduces only the four packages — 2,380 of 2,397 lines, 99.29%, which is exactly what the packages district already prints. So the verdict box and the schedule total are printed as what they are: the 2026-08-17 metering, unmoved, and labelled as not re-run. The census has since overtaken the meter in three places, daggered in the schedule; the sharpest is @tools/build_and_test, which grew 756 → 1,128 sloc when the error summary landed and lost a lamp on plate 7B, because the new code arrived with no suite behind it. The three new members are drawn dark or untethered on their first appearance: lint-elements and eslint-ts-parser have no suite at all, and warn-lanes has a real .core.test.ts that no lcov ever leaves.

REV B — the polarity is corrected, not the data. This plate's first printing drew coverage as cast shadow, so the best-tested district read gloomiest — the metaphor upside down, as the client noted: the tests are the light, and shadow should mean what shadow means. Every number below is rev A's, unchanged; only the optics flipped. The spec annex is now the lamp, covered source glows, and the members with no suite at all are finally the dark buildings they always were.

Method — one meter per member, nothing installed, nothing left behind. Footprints, annexes and districts are sheet 7 rev D's census (2026-08-31), so the two plates overlay. Light was metered on 2026-08-17 at worktree HEAD 3557c29: the four packages by the repo's own turbo run test:coverage tasks (vitest v8 for three, node --test --experimental-test-coverage + rebase-lcov for ui-router-server); the eight node:test members by the same node flags passed on the command line; @tools/happy-dom by vitest run --coverage.enabled --coverage.provider=v8. No repo file was edited. Each meter reports lines on its own basis (v8 remaps to executable lines; node counts raw lines), so brightness percentages are per-meter and never summed across meters in the drawing — only the schedule's grand total does, and says so. Lit extent uses the atlas sloc rule over the code the meter actually saw; three members' source has since moved past the metering and are daggered in the schedule.

The product glows wall to wall — the budget lands where the house says. All four published packages meter 98.1–100% line, 91.7–100% branch, over 94–100% of their source. What little stays dark is entry barrels and interface.ts — 69 of lit-ui-router's 80 shadowed sloc are type declarations, which no runtime meter can light. This is the priority made visible: library coverage outranks docs coverage, and the annexes sheet 7 drew at 1.9–3.9× their buildings turn out to buy near-total light.

The yard's habit: bright cores, dark wrappers. Four instruments repeat one pattern — @tools/release (23 .core.ts files at 98.4%, 21 CLI wrappers dark), build_and_test (3 wrappers dark), workers-builds (the 156-sloc trigger wrapper), lcov-rebase (the 17-sloc bin). 1,375 sloc of process-edge code is never loaded by any suite, while the logic behind it meters 96.7%. compat-guards is the pattern at its extreme: only ranges.ts is unit-lit; its six guard lanes run for real in CI, where no meter follows. And @tools/happy-dom is the survey's one genuine surprise, re-verified before inking: it owns a lit lamp and still stands dark, because its spec is a conformance canary pointed at happy-dom upstream — its own append.ts is lit only from lit-ui-router's lamp, as borrowed light.

What the meter cannot say, the plate refuses to fake. The two demo apps and the Cypress host are hatched in accent, not banded: e2e light is real — the rig drives the built docs site — but no lcov leaves it, so it is drawn as light of unknowable brightness and labelled unmetered. sample-app-shared runs 43 unit tests green, yet browser-mode vitest cannot fetch a coverage provider the repo never installed (@vitest/coverage-v8 is no devDependency of it); its light is an outline. Eleven members — examples, the typedoc plugin, and nine instruments — are dark because nothing tests them; two of those (dts-backtest, lit-test-env) spend their working lives inside other members' suites and are red-badged with that caveat in the schedule.
