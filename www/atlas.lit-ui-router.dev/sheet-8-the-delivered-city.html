GENERAL NOTES

Method: the production closure of apps/sample-app-lit-vanilla (dependencies plus installed peers — pnpm auto-installs peers, so they are genuinely on disk), resolved through the pnpm store and measured as delivered: lines of .js/.ts/.css/.html per package directory, source maps and prose excluded, .d.ts drawn separately as the hatched annex. Workspace-linked members are measured by their built dist/ — the shape a registry install would deliver. Plenty of prior art measures this differently — bundlephobia and packagephobia weigh tarballs and install bytes, npmgraph draws the dependency graph — but this sheet measures what actually lands on disk, in files and lines, and stages it as a city with the consumer's own building standing in it for scale. Every count on the drawing and in these notes is read at build time from the plate www/atlas.lit-ui-router.dev/data/census-nm.json — measured at origin/main @ 4dc0cbb7 (2026-10-04), closure of apps/sample-app-lit-vanilla — written after installing and building the ref itself, never the working tree. A package the drawing places that the plate does not carry, or a package the plate carries that no district draws, is a build error rather than a stale constant.

The consumer is a doormat in its own city. The app brings 646 lines of its own and receives 173,820 delivered lines of code plus 41,155 of type declarations — 269× its own mass. The router this repo exists to ship, lit-ui-router, delivers 3,152 lines over 44 files: one of the smaller buildings on the skyline it anchors, and 55× smaller than the city it stands in.

Four imports, 22,193 lines. lodash-es is delivered whole — 22,193 lines across 644 files — so the app can call isEqual, cloneDeep, get and set. The bundler tree-shakes almost all of it away, and sheet 9 charts what survives to the wire; the delivered city is the other measure, the one that installs, audits and updates. It is the fourth-tallest building here, behind dompurify, hono and @uirouter/core.

One lit, and a server in a client demo. Scoped pnpm overrides (@api-viewer/docs>lit, @api-viewer/common>lit, lit-dialog>lit → ^3.3.3) hold the demo chrome to one lit major, so lit, lit-html, lit-element and @lit/reactive-element each stand once in this city. The oddest building is hono at 24,928 lines: ui-router-server names it a peer, so peer auto-install delivers a server framework into a client demo.

Note the vertical scale: 1 px ≈ 250 lines against sheet 7's ≈ 34. Drawn at the workspace's own scale, lodash-es alone would stand 653 px tall — the compression is the finding.
