GENERAL NOTES

Method — the graph, imported. Every mass and footprint on this plate is read from www/atlas.lit-ui-router.dev/data/census-mass3b.json, the checked-in snapshot census-mass3b.mjs writes from a bare turbo run ci --dry=json on an installed archive of the ref — surveyed at origin/main @ aa7f8bfb (2026-11-14), turbo 2.11.10: 1634 nodes, 398 real, 7,042 edges, 346 real→real. Nothing below is hand-pasted; a structure whose tasks have left the plate throws at build time rather than drawing a stale number, and the schedule totals are the plate's own sums. Footprint is the per-task inputs map — the files whose hashes decide that task's cache key — at 1.2·√files per side. Height is command mass: the package.json script line plus the repo script or bin file it executes, sloc-counted by census-mass3b.mjs on scc 4.0.0's Code basis (guards, emitters and mise task files each cited in the schedule; external binaries like tsc and oxlint contribute only their one line, because that is all this repo wrote). Wall-clock and cache-hit rates are excluded as geometry by design: they are properties of runs, not of the graph.

The city is flat, and that is the finding. 311 of 398 real tasks have command mass 1 — one script line handing the work to a pinned binary. The whole city executes 7,454 sloc of repo-written command while watching 32,870 task-file hashes. The skyline is inverted from intuition: //#lint:templates, the tallest command in the city, is a 660-line check.ts watching the whole root surface of 1,818 files, and @tools/dts-backtest#test is a 396-line run.ts on a 111-file lot — while the largest footprint, the examples plain, watches 735 files (format:check:oxfmt 117 + lint:oxlint 125 alone) under 21 lines of command. The eslint-plugin quarter is nearly flat: eslint-plugin-lit-ui-router brings 10 tasks and 208 sloc, and all but build:js (87), build:types (37) and test:peer-floor (77) are one line apiece.

The root yard repays the walk. All 16 //# plots are drawn individually, and nine of them — format:check:root, lint:css, lint:package-json, lint:root, lint:templates, typecheck:root, lint:complexity, lint:doc-examples and lint:md-examples — are equal slabs watching one identical root surface of 1,818 files. Nine tasks, one surface, one number: it is the plate's cleanest finding. The guards that patrol that surface are not root tasks: @tools/repo-checks is a package, drawn whole as the guard house (1) standing apart, south-west of the yard, and eight of its 12 tasks — check:task-inputs, check:graph-edges, check:patches, check:single-version, check:package-coverage, check:knip, check:dedupe and check:shared-source — are named in the dependsOn list of the lint umbrella, so a lint run takes them along wherever it starts. Five of the eight are the city's tall one-file guards, 122, 134, 75, 85 and 114 lines of TypeScript apiece; the other three are one script line each — knip hashing 1,392 files, the widest lot in the block, pnpm dedupe --check hashing 110, and check:shared-source hashing 51. The taplo pad (11) is where plate 3A's six-hop chain touches ground: hops ①–② arrive from mise run ci, hop ③ is this block, and ④–⑥ leave immediately to run back inside mise through the cache gasket. And one lot in the yard is vacant on purpose — //#lint:workflows, an umbrella with no script of its own, has no command even here.

One tier, stated in the key. Sheet 3's severity vocabulary survives, but at this altitude it degenerates truthfully: every real node in the ci graph stops the PR when it fails, so the tier is a constant: the blocks stand in ink, the key states the tier, and the drawing spends its information elsewhere. The 6 ci:main-only tasks (test:engines ×3, check:pack, test:matrix) sit outside this survey on an unmassed accent annex — 1699/404 nodes when they are included; the 1,236 phantom plots — 75.6% of the graph, transit and ^build hash carriers and the script-less lint, typecheck and format:check umbrellas — are the vacant field behind the city, inventoried hole-by-hole on sheet 12.

Roads and reach. Five arteries are drawn, one per district pair, and the plate labels them with degree rather than with an edge tally it cannot cite: @www/lit-ui-router.dev#build waits on 32 nodes (eleven quarters ship docs:api there, plus worker types and app fixtures — it is the city's sink), @tools/typedoc-plugin-lit-ui-router#build:types unblocks 90, @tools/release#check:exports 63 (pack:all and check:exports read every published quarter), and the widest fan-out in the graph is lit-ui-router#build:types at 103. Of the graph's 7,042 edges only 346 join two real tasks; the rest hang off phantom transit nodes, which is why the roads are drawn as districts and not as a wire count.
