Method — one basis, two series. Sheet 3 throws test code away; this sheet does not — anything matching *.{spec,test,cy}.* or living under specs/ test/ tests/ __tests__/ cypress/ is counted into a second series and drawn as that member's annex. 35 members: 242 authored files and 18,927 sloc of source, plus 109 files and 16,279 sloc of spec.
