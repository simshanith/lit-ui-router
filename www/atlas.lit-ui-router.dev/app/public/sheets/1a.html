GENERAL NOTES

Method. The page is the vanilla sample app's contacts state, /app/contacts and then one contact at /app/contacts/fruiz, served from its built dist and measured in headless Chromium at 1280 × 900 and 390 × 844 after document.fonts.ready settles: getBoundingClientRect for every box, getComputedStyle for every padding, margin, border, radius and flex. The visualizer and the API docs are switched off, so the routed page stands alone. census-document.mjs writes the readings to census-document.json, reads each declared rule out of styles.css at origin/main @ 3f55e419, and stops when a selector is missing or a formula misses the page by half a pixel. The build is this branch's at eca2d156, whose apps/ and packages/ match 3f55e419 file for file.

One geometry for four apps. The four sample apps take their stylesheet and their pages from sample-app-shared, and their 4 built stylesheets are byte-identical (6,128 bytes, sha-256 2575db0b00a9e455…). Each app writes its own shell, and the 4 shells carry the same markup: a nav of uiSref tabs and three <ui-view> slots in <main>. The shared pages hold four more, the root view and the nested ones inside contacts and messages. The tab padding is Bootstrap 3.3.7's, which the apps load from a CDN; every other spacing on the plate is the shared stylesheet's.

What the drawing says. Under the nav, the shell's named <ui-view name="contacts"> fills the page. Inside it the contact list carries no width of its own: it hugs its longest name at W1 = 176.31 px at both widths, and its height is a sum, H6 = 2·S6 + H7 + LH + N·H2, over 25 rows. The nested <ui-view> is unnamed and flexes into the rest of the row, W2 = VW − W1; the contact's content sits inset in it by S9 and S10, and every link on the page — tab, row, action — carries uiSref and hugs its label. At 390 the nav wraps to two rows, H4 = H1 + H3 − 2·M1 + B, and the list row wraps too: the nested view drops under the list and takes the full line, so the page grows to H10 below a fold at VH2.

Sheet 1's plates are this page's layers. Sheet 1 raises its document as plates from the window to the link; the stack at the lower right is the same climb, measured: window, body, #root, <ui-router>, the root <ui-view>, the shell's named view, the nested view and the active row's link, each at its real rectangle and its depth in the DOM. A layer stack is honest here because the page really is layered. Sheet 1 letters its window /people with a named detail view; the sample app's own names are contacts for the view the shell fills and none for the nested one the detail lands in.
