GENERAL NOTES

Method: the production closure of apps/sample-app-lit-vanilla (dependencies plus required peers — pnpm auto-installs those, so they are genuinely on disk; an optional peer arrives only where the consumer adds it), resolved through the pnpm store and measured as delivered: lines of .js/.ts/.css/.html per package directory, source maps and prose excluded, .d.ts drawn separately as the hatched annex. Workspace-linked members are measured by their built dist/ — the shape a registry install would deliver. Plenty of prior art measures this differently — bundlephobia and packagephobia weigh tarballs and install bytes, npmgraph draws the dependency graph — but this sheet measures what actually lands on disk, in files and lines, and stages it as a city with the consumer's own building standing in it for scale. Every count on the drawing and in these notes is read at build time from the plate www/atlas.lit-ui-router.dev/data/census-nm.json — measured at origin/main @ 6ae72d27 (2026-11-10), closure of apps/sample-app-lit-vanilla — written after installing and building the ref itself, never the working tree. A package the drawing places that the plate does not carry, or a package the plate carries that no district draws, is a build error rather than a stale constant.

The consumer is a doormat in its own city. The app brings 646 lines of its own and receives 156,321 delivered lines of code plus 36,990 of type declarations — 242× its own mass. The router this repo exists to ship, lit-ui-router, delivers 3,653 lines over 50 files: one of the smaller buildings on the skyline it anchors, and 43× smaller than the city it stands in.

Four imports, 22,193 lines. lodash-es is delivered whole — 22,193 lines across 644 files — so the app can call isEqual, cloneDeep, get and set. The bundler tree-shakes almost all of it away, and sheet 9 charts what survives to the wire; the delivered city is the other measure, the one that installs, audits and updates. It is the third-tallest building here, behind dompurify and @uirouter/core.

One lit, and a server router with one dependency. Scoped pnpm overrides (@api-viewer/docs>lit, @api-viewer/common>lit, lit-dialog>lit → ^3.3.3) hold the demo chrome to one lit major, so lit, lit-html, lit-element and @lit/reactive-element each stand once in this city. ui-router-server brings one registry dependency of its own, escape-html, at 79 lines; its optional peers hono and vite are a consumer's to add, so they stand outside this city.

Note the vertical scale: 1 px ≈ 250 lines against sheet 7's ≈ 34. Drawn at the workspace's own scale, lodash-es alone would stand 653 px tall — the compression is the finding.
