APPENDIX · META — the sheet every plate is set in, as a dimensional drawing: the double frame, the head, the plate and its fill button, the schedule, the caption, the notes and the title block, in codes · 33 instances, one geometry · measured at www/atlas @ eca2d156, outside the cabinet

GENERAL NOTES

Method — the built app, measured. generator/survey-chrome.mjs serves the built app and reads one routed sheet, /sheet/7/, the cover and the bare plate in chromium at 1440 and 390 wide, after document.fonts.ready: every box the chrome draws, by getBoundingClientRect and getComputedStyle. A constant the CSS cannot show, such as the schedule split SCHEDULE_SPLIT, and every clamp expression are read off chrome.mjs's own source and filed as their own rows. A missing selector stops the probe; the cover's frame and the routed sheet's must agree at both widths. The plate is data/survey-chrome.json, 75 rows of 57 codes at www/atlas @ eca2d156: the atlas is not on main, so the plate stands outside the cabinet, as the other self-portraits do.

Codes, not numbers. The drawing letters codes; the values stand in the schedules. S is spacing, B a border or rule, W a width, H a height, Z a square, T a lettering size, G a grid track or count. A derived row carries its formula: the inner rule stands 9 px in from the outer edge, B + S1. A row the two widths agree on is one row; a row they part on is two, one per width.

What the drawing says. The chrome declares a 1.5 px frame on the sheet, the schedules, the caption's rule and the title block, and chromium draws each at 1 px at a device pixel ratio of 2; the inner rule's offset follows the frame it is drawn by. Every corner is square. At 390 wide the head wraps to 67.53 px against 34.28, the title steps down its clamp from 24 to 18 px, the drawing keeps its 1000 px floor and scrolls inside a 320 px wrap, and the key leaves its sticky seat to stand under the notes.
