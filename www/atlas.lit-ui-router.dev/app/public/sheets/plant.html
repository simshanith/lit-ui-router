BASIS The model masses sheet 7's own geometry: every footprint, height and position is generator/sheet7.mjs's computed CITY export, embedded verbatim as JSON and massed from data/census-city.json, origin/main @ 4dc0cbb7 (2026-10-04). Nothing is re-derived, so a mass in the model cannot drift from the mass on the plate. three.js 0.169.0 is imported only once the plate scrolls into view, and the scene renders on demand: nothing runs while you read.

PAPER The masses are drawn the way the flat plates draw them, on paper that is never quite opaque: the cap at 88% and the walls at 80%, so the girding frame and the plant behind a wall show through it, the frame at 30% of its stroke. The cap takes the tier's own fill; each right-hand wall takes the tier's hatch over a --paper-2 stone, the tier's hue pulled 22% of the way in so the tiers still part at a glance. Each frame strokes the tier's edge colour from the ladder the plates use (red, accent, --line, soft, ink). Only the colour travels; a WebGL line carries no width.

HATCH Laid in screen space: one rake, one spacing, on every wall at every angle, which is what patternUnits="userSpaceOnUse" means on the flat set. It is a stripe mixed into the fragment colour off gl_FragCoord, so it costs no texture and no dependency. Gate severity is the rake: the halt and PR hatch runs opposite to the neutral one, and the halt cap is filled red. The pr and late tiers carry sheet 7's roof wash, the cap taking the side's hatch. The off tier is drawn frame-only, because there is nothing to mass.

PLANT Every massed member is drawn as a working plant, after the sprite study's Factorio-leaning treatment, and every piece of it reads a field the census row already carries. The plant keeps to the roof's corners, clear of the number chip over its centre. Stacks stand in a row up the west edge, one per ten authored files up to four, their height set by the footprint and banded in the tier's edge colour. Tanks line the north edge from the north-east corner, one at 150 sloc, two at 600, three at 1,800, as many as the roof holds. A header pipe joins them, and the field left over carries one vent per five files, up to six. A footprint of 45 units or more takes a portal gantry along its east edge, and one of 28 or more a catwalk rail. A wall of 20 units or more runs a riser up its east face, two from 60, and a mass of 40 or more is ringed by a deck every 30 units. A spec annex is joined by a pipe rack across the gap and carries three module lamps, lit green by sheet 7A's line coverage: three at 95 and over, two at 85, one below or unmetered, none when no suite loads the member. Where a choice is left, such as which roof cells the vents take, a generator seeded on the member's name makes it, so the same census always builds the same plant. The plant is one merged mesh, shaded per vertex and edged in ink.

LETTERING Each src mass carries a billboarded chip with sheet 7's own number, drawn at runtime into a canvas in the page's mono stack and redrawn when the theme turns. Chips drop out below zoom 0.62, so a pulled-back plan stays a plan. District names lie flat on their ground plates, turned onto the opening diagonal: level at rest, foreshortened with the ground as a site plan's lettering is. The shadow slab is sheet 7A's own black wash with a faint ink stripe, lerped toward black rather than the ink, because --ink is light in the cyanotype theme and a shadow that brightens in the dark is not a shadow.
