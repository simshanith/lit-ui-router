GENERAL NOTES

Method. The page follows page 1.6 of the NASA Graphics Standards Manual (NHB 1430.2, January 1976, Danne & Blackburn, in NASA's later reprint, PDF), whose incorrect uses draw nine wrong logotypes, number them and name each in a sentence. Here the subject is the atlas's own drawing rules: each rule is read at build time from the file that states it, docs/ISOMETRIC.md or docs/COPY.md, and each guard from the file that holds it, so a rule whose wording moves, or a guard that loses its check, stops the build. The counts of sheets calling assertPlots and solidFaces are read off the sheet modules the same way.

The pairs. 1, lettering on a mass face, and 2, a mass past the viewBox, are two of the three counts of generator/audit-frames.mjs, which exits 1 on any. 3, overlapping footprints, throws in assertPlots on the 12 sheets that call it. 4, a face written as stroke and fill in one polygon, draws no fill, because each stroke class declares fill: none and the class outranks the attribute; the rear block's edges read through the front one. solidFaces rewrites such a face on the 7 sheets that call it, and stops nothing; face() is the correct form. 9, a count written by hand, is held sheet by sheet with a throw against the plate, sheet 3B's TALLEST among them. 5, a solid hairline leader, 6, a dash drawn as a hidden edge, 7, accent on five elements, and 8, a title box inside the drawing, have no guard: the rules stand in docs/ISOMETRIC.md and in review alone, and these 4 are the candidates.

The plate keeps its own rules. The frame audit reads this plate as it reads the others, so lettering inside a panel is drawn as two rules standing for a line of text: pair 1's incorrect panel sets its rules on the block's face, a fault the audit cannot see because no text is there. Pair 2's block is cut by a drawn frame standing for the viewBox, inside the real one. Pair 4's incorrect panel is written the incorrect way, a stroke class and a fill attribute on one polygon. Pair 7 is the one place the plate spends accent: five caps in the incorrect panel, one cap and its callout in the correct one, and the key names that meaning. The third count of the audit, text-on-text, and the history words the atlas-copy skill greps for by hand stand outside the nine.
