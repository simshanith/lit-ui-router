GENERAL NOTES

Up from the family. Sheet 2 seats the family on @uirouter/core; sheet 2C goes down to the runtimes the family peers. This plate goes up: the sample apps, the docs site, the embedded examples and the atlas's own app, each massed at sheet 2's mould from its authored source — the city ruler's files under its src/ (the docs site's .vitepress/, worker/ and src/), specs aside — and seated on the parts its manifest pins.

One hub. Nineteen of the twenty-one consumers pin lit-ui-router, and every one of them hovers over it, since it is the heaviest part each pins: the builds split the nineteen into their panels, and every build but SERVER stands its consumers on brick 1. Of the other parts, ui-router-registry is pinned by four, ui-router-navigation-location-plugin is pinned by three, ui-router-server is pinned by three, lit-ui-router-effect is pinned by three, lit-ui-router-signals is pinned by three, lit-ui-router-mobx is pinned by two, lit-ui-router-ssr is pinned by two, ui-router-effect is pinned by two, ui-router-signals is pinned by two, eslint-plugin-lit-ui-router is pinned by two and ui-router-mobx is pinned by one.

Cords. A consumer seats on the heaviest part it pins and reaches each other part by a string with end studs, a 1×1 round plate at either end and a cord between. Every consumer hovers, so a string hangs from a free stud of its underside where the cord drops clear, and otherwise leaves a ringed stud of its top; the far plate seats on a ringed stud of the pinned part. The sheet draws 26 cords, one for every pin past a consumer's seat: twenty hang, six leave the top, and where no straight cord passes every mass above or in front of it the string bends and winds round what stands in its way, as one do — altitude-atlas-app to brick 11. Brick 5, brick 3 and brick 9 have fewer free studs than cords to take, so two cords share one round plate there.

The examples are thin. An embedded example is a file or two under examples/<key>/src/, so most mould a single course; sample-app-shared, at 43 files, stands 15 courses tall.
