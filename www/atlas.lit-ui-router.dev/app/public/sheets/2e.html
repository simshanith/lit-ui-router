GENERAL NOTES

Up from the family. Sheet 2 seats the family on @uirouter/core; sheet 2C goes down to the runtimes the family peers. This plate goes up: the sample apps, the docs site, the embedded examples and the atlas's own app, each massed at sheet 2's mould from its authored source — the city ruler's files under its src/ (the docs site's .vitepress/, worker/ and src/), specs aside — and seated on the parts its manifest pins.

One hub. Nineteen of the twenty-one consumers pin lit-ui-router, it is the heaviest part each pins, and eighteen of them hover over it; altitude-atlas-app hovers over brick 2, ui-router-navigation-location-plugin, since from over brick 1 no cord to it runs clear: the builds split the nineteen into their panels, and every build but SERVER and THE ATLAS stands its consumers on brick 1. Of the other parts, ui-router-registry is pinned by ten, ui-router-navigation-location-plugin is pinned by three, ui-router-server is pinned by three, lit-ui-router-effect is pinned by three, lit-ui-router-signals is pinned by three, lit-ui-router-mobx is pinned by two, lit-ui-router-ssr is pinned by two, ui-router-effect is pinned by two, ui-router-signals is pinned by two, eslint-plugin-lit-ui-router is pinned by two and ui-router-mobx is pinned by one.

Cords. A consumer seats on the heaviest part it pins — or, where no cord runs clear from over it, on the pinned part that lets every cord run — and reaches each other part by a string with end studs, a 1×1 round plate at either end and a cord between. Every consumer hovers, so a string hangs from a free stud of its underside where the cord drops clear, and otherwise leaves a ringed stud of its top; the far plate seats on a ringed stud of the pinned part. The sheet draws 32 cords, one for every pin past a consumer's seat: twenty-one hang, eleven leave the top. Brick 7, brick 9, brick 5 and brick 3 have fewer free studs than cords to take, so two cords share one round plate there.

The examples are thin. An embedded example is a file or two under examples/<key>/src/, so most mould a single course; sample-app-shared, at 44 files, stands 15 courses tall.
