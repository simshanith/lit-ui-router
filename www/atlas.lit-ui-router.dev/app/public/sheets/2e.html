GENERAL NOTES

Up from the family. Sheet 2 seats the family on @uirouter/core; sheet 2C goes down to the runtimes the family peers. This plate goes up: the sample apps, the docs site, the embedded examples and the atlas's own app, each massed at sheet 2's mould from its authored source — the city ruler's files under its src/ (the docs site's .vitepress/, worker/ and src/), specs aside — and seated on the parts its manifest pins.

One hub. Sixteen of the nineteen consumers pin lit-ui-router, and every one of them hovers over it, since it is the heaviest part each pins: the builds split the sixteen into their panels, and every build but SERVER stands its consumers on brick 1. Of the other parts, ui-router-navigation-location-plugin is pinned by three, ui-router-server is pinned by three, lit-ui-router-effect is pinned by three, lit-ui-router-signals is pinned by three, lit-ui-router-mobx is pinned by two, ui-router-signals is pinned by two, eslint-plugin-lit-ui-router is pinned by two, lit-ui-router-ssr is pinned by one and ui-router-effect is pinned by one.

Cords. A consumer seats on the heaviest part it pins and reaches each other part by a string with end studs, a 1×1 round plate seated on a ringed stud at either end and a cord between. The sheet draws 15 cords; three pins find no cord that passes every mass above or in front of it — hellosolarsystem-signals to brick 9, altitude-atlas-app to brick 5 and altitude-atlas-app to brick 8 — and the schedule lists them undrawn. Brick 7 and brick 2 have fewer free studs than cords to take, so two cords share one round plate there.

The examples are thin. An embedded example is a file or two under examples/<key>/src/, so most mould a single course; sample-app-shared, at 41 files, stands 14 courses tall.
