GENERAL NOTES

Method — the graph, imported. Every mass and footprint on this plate is read from www/atlas.lit-ui-router.dev/data/census-mass3b.json, the checked-in snapshot census-mass3b.mjs writes from a bare turbo run ci --dry=json on an installed archive of the ref — surveyed at origin/main @ 38c9fa1c (2026-09-23), turbo 2.11.2: 716 nodes, 225 real, 1,897 edges, 111 real→real. Nothing below is hand-pasted; a structure whose tasks have left the plate throws at build time rather than drawing a stale number, and the schedule totals are the plate's own sums. Footprint is the per-task inputs map — the files whose hashes decide that task's cache key — at 1.2·√files per side. Height is command mass: the package.json script line plus the repo script or bin file it executes, sloc-counted by census-mass3b.mjs on scc 4.0.0's Code basis (guards, emitters and mise task files each cited in the schedule; external binaries like tsc and oxlint contribute only their one line, because that is all this repo wrote). Wall-clock and cache-hit rates are excluded as geometry by design: they are properties of runs, not of the graph.

The city is flat, and that is the finding. 177 of 225 real tasks have command mass 1 — one script line handing the work to a pinned binary. The whole city executes 2,656 sloc of repo-written command while watching 11,701 task-file hashes. The skyline is inverted from intuition: @tools/dts-backtest#test is a 293-line run.ts on a 38-file lot — sheet 3 calls it "one 291-line run.ts holds the TS 5.0 floor", and the graph survey agrees to the line — while the largest footprint, the examples plain, watches 303 files (format:check 61 + lint 61 alone) under 19 lines of command. The tallest command is the root yard's //#lint:elements, the one root lane that runs a repo-written bin rather than an external one — 186 sloc over warn-lanes.core.ts's 214 — a 401-sloc spire on a root-surface footprint. The eslint-plugin quarter is flat throughout: eslint-plugin-lit-ui-router brings 9 tasks and 85 sloc, and all but its two oxc-emit build lanes are one line apiece.

The root yard repays the walk. All 13 //# plots are drawn individually, and six of them — lint:root, typecheck:root, lint:elements, format:check:root, lint:templates and lint:package-json — are equal slabs watching one identical root surface of 818 files. Six tasks, one surface, one number: it is the plate's cleanest finding. The guards that patrol that surface are not root tasks: @tools/repo-checks is a package, drawn whole as the guard house (1) standing off the yard's west edge, and four of its 8 tasks — check:graph-edges, check:task-inputs, check:patches and check:knip — are named in the lint task's with list, so a lint run drags them along wherever it starts. Three of the four are the city's tall one-file guards, 124, 97 and 70 lines of TypeScript apiece; the fourth is one knip line hashing 630 files, the widest lot in the block. The taplo pad (12) is where plate 3A's six-hop chain touches ground: hops ①–② arrive from mise run ci, hop ③ is this block, and ④–⑥ leave immediately to run back inside mise through the cache gasket. And one lot in the yard is vacant on purpose — //#lint:workflows, the virtual with twin, has no command even here.

One tier, uniformly red. Sheet 3's severity vocabulary survives, but at this altitude it degenerates truthfully: every real node in the ci graph stops the PR when it fails, so every massed block wears the same red hatch, and the drawing spends its information elsewhere. The 5 ci:main-only tasks (test:engines ×3, check:pack, test:matrix) sit outside this survey on an unmassed accent annex — 756/230 nodes when they are included; the 491 phantom plots — 68.6% of the graph, transit and ^build hash carriers — are the vacant field behind the city, inventoried hole-by-hole on sheet 12.

Roads and reach. Five arteries are drawn, one per district pair, and the plate labels them with degree rather than with an edge tally it cannot cite: @www/lit-ui-router.dev#build waits on 18 nodes (every quarter ships docs:api there, plus worker types and app fixtures — it is the city's sink), @tools/typedoc-plugin-lit-ui-router#build:types unblocks 36, @tools/release#check:exports 38 (pack:all and check:exports read every quarter), and the widest fan-out in the graph is lit-ui-router#build:types at 50. Of the graph's 1,897 edges only 111 join two real tasks; the rest hang off phantom transit nodes, which is why the roads are drawn as districts and not as a wire count.
